# Tollhaven Trouser Co. Shopify product page section

- **Category:** Shopify
- **Label:** Concept demo
- **Year:** 2026

## What it is

An Online Store 2.0 product section for a made-up trouser brand that sells one chino in four colors, six waists and three lengths. The size guide sits next to the size picker, length is its own choice, sold-out and never-made sizes look different, a delivery window sits under the price, and on a phone the add-to-cart button follows you down the page.

## Who it's for

Shopify apparel shops that get a lot of "what size?" messages and returns, and anyone asking for a custom product page, a size chart, or a Liquid section they can edit in the theme editor without an app.

## Key features

- Size guide next to the size option, with a size finder and separate body and garment tables, in or cm
- Short, Regular and Long as their own option, with the inseam on each
- Sold-out sizes crossed out with a back-in-stock form; combinations that are never made marked differently
- Delivery window under the price from the warehouse cutoff, weekends and closed days
- Sticky add-to-cart bar on phones that hides at the footer
- Every label and rule is a theme editor setting; 33 automated tests, and the demo is the section rendered from its Liquid

## Honesty

Concept demo. Tollhaven Trouser Co., The Field Chino, its prices and stock, and the contact details (555-01xx, example.com) are made up. Tested offline with LiquidJS and headless Chrome; not installed on a real Shopify store. Not client work, and no sales or results are claimed.
