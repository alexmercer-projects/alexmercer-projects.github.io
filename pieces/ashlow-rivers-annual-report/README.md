# Ashlow Rivers Trust annual review

- **Category:** Print design
- **Label:** Concept demo
- **Year:** 2026

## What it is

A print-ready 16-page A4 annual review for a made-up UK rivers charity, plus a web page where you can browse the spreads, look at the layout system and download the PDF.

## Who it's for

Charities, nonprofits and anyone who needs a designed annual report, review or illustrated publication they can also share online.

## Key features

- 16-page A4 print-ready tagged PDF, with maps, charts and figures drawn as SVG
- A web viewer that shows the report as booklet spreads, with a keyboard-friendly page reader
- Notes on the page grid, type scale, colour palette and contrast
- Every figure marked as example data, and a fictional charity number on the finances page

## Honesty

Concept demo. The trust, places, people and every number are made up. Not client work, and no results are claimed.
