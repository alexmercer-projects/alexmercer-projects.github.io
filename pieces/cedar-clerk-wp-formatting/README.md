# Cedar & Clerk: WordPress article formatting

- **Category:** WordPress / content formatting
- **Label:** Concept demo
- **Year:** 2026

## What it is

A finished-looking article, formatted as it would be on a WordPress site, plus a reusable checklist for the rest of a batch. The made-up publication is Cedar & Clerk. The sample post is "How to set up a weekend farmers market stall without overbuying." A sidebar stands in for the WordPress document and SEO panels (slug, SEO title, meta description, categories, tags, featured image, internal links).

## Who it's for

WordPress posting and formatting gigs, "get my drafts live" asks, editorial assistants who need clean headings, images, slugs and SEO fields, and anyone who wants a formatting checklist for a mixed batch of unpublished, unposted and still-being-written articles.

A rate already used for this kind of work: $20 per article, or $360 flat for a first batch of 20, when copy and images are ready.

## Key features

- Clean H2/H3 structure and a readable long-form layout
- Featured image with alt text (1400x788, compressed)
- SEO title (54/60) and meta description (146/155)
- Categories, tags, a clean slug, and three descriptive internal links
- Page 2 of the PDF is a formatting checklist, including a mixed-batch order
- `print.html` is the print/PDF source

## Honesty

Concept demo. Cedar & Clerk is not a live publication. Maya Ellison is a placeholder byline. `cedarandclerk.example` is a fake domain. The article is placeholder content, not a client's draft. The sidebar is a stand-in for wp-admin, not a screenshot of a real WordPress install. Do not imply a live client site.
