# Facebook posting plan for a painting company

- **Category:** Ads / social
- **Label:** Concept demo
- **Year:** 2026

## What it is

A one-page organic Facebook plan for a painting company, with a posting cadence, a page checklist, and five ready-to-use post drafts (before/after, job story, honest tip, behind the scenes, seasonal CTA). Business name, colors and job details are bracketed placeholders. The example service area is Boise and the Treasure Valley.

## Who it's for

Facebook posting and page-management gigs for trades and home services, especially painters and other local contractors who want posts they can actually publish, not just a strategy memo.

## Key features

- Four rotating post types
- 2-3 posts a week, with a batching habit
- Page checklist (logo, cover, call button, recent work, recommendations)
- Five fill-in drafts a crew could post after swapping in photos and a color name

## Honesty

Concept demo / example plan. The business name is a placeholder. This is not a campaign that ran, and there are no reach, lead or booking results. Do not attach it to a real painting company.
