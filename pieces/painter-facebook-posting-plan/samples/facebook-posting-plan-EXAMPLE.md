# Facebook Posting Plan: [Business Name] Painting (Boise / Treasure Valley)
*Free sample, one page. Bracketed items get filled in once you send your business name, page link and main job types.*

## The goal
Every post should do one of two things: show your work, or show you're someone a homeowner can trust in their house. Skip the generic "Call us today!" graphics; people scroll past them.

## What to post (rotate these 4)
1. **Before/after.** Two photos from the same angle, plus one line on what changed. These work best, so aim for one a week.
2. **Job story.** 3–4 sentences: the problem, what you did, how it turned out. Name the area ("a 1970s ranch in West Boise"), never the client's address.
3. **Honest tip.** Something homeowners actually wonder about: sheen choices, when it's too cold to paint exteriors, why prep takes longer than painting.
4. **Behind the scenes.** You or the crew taping, prepping, cleaning up. It shows care, and care is what gets you hired.

## How often and when
- **2–3 posts a week** is enough. Steady beats a lot of posts at once.
- Try weekday evenings (about 6–8 PM) and Saturday mornings, when homeowners are scrolling. After a month, check Page Insights to see what actually worked for your page.
- Batch it: shoot photos on every job, then spend 20 minutes Sunday scheduling the week's posts in Meta Business Suite (free).

## Where to post
- **Your Business Page** first, always.
- **Local community and neighborhood groups** (Boise, Meridian, Nampa, Eagle, Kuna). Read each group's rules before posting; many only allow business posts on a set "promo day." The full plan includes the vetted group list with each group's rules.
- **Reply fast** to every comment and message. A quick answer often wins the job.

## 5 ready-to-use post drafts

**1. Before/after (exterior)**
> Before → after on this [West Boise] home. Faded, peeling trim and siding, now [color name] with crisp white trim. Two coats, full prep, and the homeowner says the street looks different. Thinking about your exterior before winter? Message us for a free estimate. 📸 [before] [after]

**2. Job story (interior)**
> This [Meridian] living room had [dark accent walls / water-stained ceiling] the owners had lived with for years. We patched, primed and went with [color] in an eggshell finish. It took a day and a half, furniture went back where it was, and the room finally feels finished. [photo]

**3. Honest tip**
> Quick tip: flat, eggshell or satin? Flat hides wall flaws but scuffs easily, so it's great for ceilings. Eggshell is the go-to for living rooms and bedrooms. Satin or semi-gloss is for kitchens, baths and trim, where you'll be wiping things down. Questions about your project? Drop them below 👇

**4. Behind the scenes**
> The part nobody posts about: prep. Before a drop of paint goes on we [move and cover furniture, fill holes, sand, caulk, tape]. It's most of the job, and it's why the finish lasts. [photo of prep]

**5. Seasonal / call to action**
> Booking [December/January] interior jobs now. Winter is a great time for inside work: bedrooms, cabinets, ceilings, trim. Serving Boise, Meridian, Nampa and nearby. Send us a message with a photo of the room and we'll get back to you with a quote.
