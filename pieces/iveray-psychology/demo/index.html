Meet Nell Iveray

Dr. Iveray works with adults who are anxious, grieving, or in the middle of a change they didn’t plan for. Sessions are straightforward and unrushed. You do not need to arrive with a speech.

We’ll talk about what’s happening now, and also the patterns that keep showing up. She draws on a few well-known therapy approaches and will tell you what she’s thinking as you go.

This bio is written for the demo. Nell Iveray is not a real clinician, and the credentials below are placeholders.
