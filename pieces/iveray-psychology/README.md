# Iveray Psychology

- **Category:** Website
- **Label:** Concept demo
- **Year:** 2026

## What it is

A one-page site for a made-up clinical psychology practice in a fictional town called Fellbrook. It covers about, intake through an outside portal (nothing health-related is collected on the page), a booking placeholder, office and telehealth details, and a 988 crisis line in the footer.

## Who it's for

Small professional-service websites, especially therapy or wellness practices that should not take health information through the site itself.

## Key features

- Calm Home/About with a short bio, areas of focus and clearly labelled placeholder credentials
- A Complete intake button that points off-site, not to a form on this page
- A booking placeholder plus office and telehealth information
- A 988 crisis line in the footer, and a note asking people not to send health information through the site
- Works on a phone, with self-hosted fonts

## Honesty

Concept demo. Iveray Psychology and Dr. Nell Iveray are not real. Names, credentials, the address, the phone number and the email are placeholders. The site does not collect health information, and the intake button and booking times are for show.
