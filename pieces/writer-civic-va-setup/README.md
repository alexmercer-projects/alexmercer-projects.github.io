# Week-1 VA setup for a writer and civic lead

- **Category:** VA / operations
- **Label:** Concept demo
- **Year:** 2026

## What it is

A week-1 operating setup that turns voice notes into tasks, stages publishing work, and keeps civic follow-ups from slipping. It includes a simple tracker (idea to next physical step), a 3-line async daily check-in, inbox labels, a publishing checklist and calendar rules. The principal is fictional.

## Who it's for

Executive or personal VA asks, "turn my voice notes into tasks," inbox triage, editorial calendars, and light operations support for a writer, newsletter author or civic volunteer.

## Key features

- Capture lane: same-day transcription of voice memos into tasks
- Editorial calendar and a staging checklist for newsletter, blog and video
- Civic intake and a follow-up log with a 7-day promise rule
- Inbox labels (reply today, waiting on, archive)
- A short written daily check-in format

## Honesty

Concept demo. The writer, the civic work, and every name, date and tool choice are examples. This is a starting system, not a finished client engagement, and it is not based on a live client's private tools or accounts. Do not present it as a case study of a real person.
