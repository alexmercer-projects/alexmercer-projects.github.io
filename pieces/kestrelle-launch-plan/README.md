# Kestrelle UAE launch plan

- **Category:** Marketing
- **Label:** Concept demo
- **Year:** 2026

## What it is

An 8-week marketing launch plan for a made-up skincare brand opening in the UAE, starting with Dubai. It goes from positioning and audience through channels, a week-by-week timeline, a launch-week content calendar, creator outreach, an example budget, KPIs and a test plan.

## Who it's for

Brands launching a product or entering a new market, DTC and e-commerce teams that need a go-to-market plan, and anyone who wants to see how I'd lay one out before handing over their own brief.

## Key features

- Market snapshot built only from public sources, with every figure linked, plus a separate box of assumptions to check
- Positioning statement, three audience sketches, message pillars and words to avoid
- Channel plan with one job per channel and what to watch
- 8-week timeline with the launch in week 5 and the expected Ramadan window marked
- Launch-week content calendar, day by day
- Creator tiers, outreach steps, a sample first message and the brief checklist
- Example AED 75,000 budget split, KPIs with example targets and three tests with clear rules
- The whole plan as a 10-page A4 PDF

## Honesty

Concept demo. Kestrelle, its products, prices, creators, budget and targets are made up. The market facts are real and sourced on the page. Not client work, nothing in it has run, and no results are claimed.
