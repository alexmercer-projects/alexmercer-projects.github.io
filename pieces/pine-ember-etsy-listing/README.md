# Pine & Ember: Etsy listing makeover

- **Category:** E-commerce copy / listings
- **Label:** Concept demo
- **Year:** 2026

## What it is

A before-and-after Etsy listing rewrite for a made-up shop, Pine & Ember, selling a pine-forest soy candle. A thin "Candle" listing is rebuilt to Etsy's published limits: title, 13 tags, description, care and shipping, and a photo plan. Checks are simple rule checks, not sales results.

## Who it's for

Etsy and marketplace listing gigs, product-copy rewrites, and sellers who want a clearer title, tags and description.

## Key features

- Side-by-side before and after
- Title length and keyword placement vs Etsy's 140-character limit
- 13 tags, each 20 characters or under, mostly long-tail
- Description with what you get, scent notes, care and shipping
- A 7-photo shot list
- Rule score improving from 1/9 to 9/9

## Honesty

Demo example. Pine & Ember is not a real shop. No sales, conversion or ranking results are claimed. The checks follow Etsy's published listing limits only.
