# Hollis & Pike Home Repair

- **Category:** Website
- **Label:** Concept demo
- **Year:** 2026

## What it is

A one-page site for a made-up handyman and appliance repair business in a fictional town (Sable Creek). It is built to get a call or a photo quote: tap-to-call at the top, starting prices for each kind of job, a service-area map, how booking works, a quote form, hours and an FAQ.

## Who it's for

Plumber, handyman, electrician and contractor website asks, home-services landing pages, and local-business rebuilds (WordPress, Squarespace, Wix or plain HTML) where the goal is more calls, not a brand manifesto.

## Key features

- Sticky call button on phones
- Posted starting prices by job type (appliance repair, leaks, doors, drywall, and more)
- Four-step booking path and a photo quote form
- Service-area map
- Hours and FAQ
- Self-hosted fonts (Archivo, IBM Plex Mono)

## Honesty

Concept demo. The business does not exist. Names, prices, hours and contact details are placeholders. Use a 555-style phone number if you show contact details. Do not claim calls, bookings or ranking results.
