FAQ

Before you call.

Do you charge to come look at something?

Quotes from photos are free. Appliances need hands on them, so there’s an $89 diagnostic, and it comes off the bill if you go ahead with the repair.

What counts as too big a job?

Anything that needs a permit, a new circuit, moving plumbing or more than a day of work. We’ll tell you straight away and point you to the right trade.

Do I need to be home?

For a first visit, yes, or have someone there who can show us the problem. After that, a lockbox code is fine for simple jobs.

Who buys the parts?

We do, and bill them at cost with the receipt attached. If you already have the part, we’ll install yours.

What if the fix doesn’t hold?

If the same repair fails within 90 days, we come back and redo it at no charge.

Can you come today?

Sometimes. Call before 10am and ask about open windows. Same-day slots go first to leaks and appliances that hold food.
