Alderbend Landscape Co.

About

A small crew that likes finishing things.

Alderbend is a made-up landscaping company, invented to show what a WordPress theme can do for a small trade business. In the story, it is five people who design, build and look after yards in six towns.

The way they work is the point of the site: one crew, a written quote, a fixed route day, and honest answers when a job is not worth doing.

  • We build what we drew, or tell you why we changed it.
  • Quotes list materials and labour separately.
  • We clean up every day, not just at the end.

Where we work

Six towns, one route day each.

Keeping to a fixed day per town means the crew is never an hour from your yard when something needs a look. Type your town to see ours.

  • Millbrook Ridge Monday
  • Harrow Landing Tuesday
  • Ketterby Wednesday
