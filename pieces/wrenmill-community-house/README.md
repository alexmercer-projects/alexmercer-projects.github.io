# Wrenmill Community House

- **Category:** Website
- **Label:** Concept demo
- **Year:** 2026

## What it is

A one-page homepage for a made-up small-town nonprofit and community hall. It shows this week's events, ongoing programs, hall rental, volunteer sign-up and a donate block. Built to show the kind of site that fits a community organization, not a store.

## Who it's for

Nonprofit, church, community-center and hall-rental website asks, and small organizations that need events, programs and a donate path on one page.

## Key features

- This week's events
- Programs on the calendar
- Sample hall rates and desk hours
- Volunteer and donate blocks
- Phone-friendly layout
- Self-hosted fonts (Crimson Pro, Libre Franklin)

## Honesty

Concept demo. Wrenmill Community House is not a real organization. Names, times, prices and contact details are placeholders, including the 555 number. Do not claim donations, attendance or a real location.
