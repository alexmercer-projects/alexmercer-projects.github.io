# Ordinary Mechanics: YouTube explainer script

- **Category:** Writing / video
- **Label:** Concept demo
- **Year:** 2026

## What it is

A full timed YouTube explainer script for a made-up channel, on why your ears pop on planes (about 7 and a half minutes). It includes title and thumbnail options, a 15-second hook, a two-column voiceover-and-visuals script, a paste-ready description with chapters, and a source for every fact. The video was never produced.

## Who it's for

YouTube explainer and mini-doc script asks, scriptwriter or researcher roles, and health or science explainers that need citations.

## Key features

- Title and thumbnail packages, with one marked as the pick
- Breakdown of the first 15 seconds
- Timed two-column script (voiceover with source numbers next to visuals)
- Description, chapters, pinned comment, cards, end screen and tags
- Sourced facts
- One-page PDF of the script pack

## Honesty

Concept demo. The channel does not exist and the video was never made, so there are no view, click or retention numbers. Do not invent them.
