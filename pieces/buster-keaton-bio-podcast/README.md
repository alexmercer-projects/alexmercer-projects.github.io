# Buster Keaton: IMDb-style mini bio and podcast structure

- **Category:** Writing / media
- **Label:** Spec sample (public figure)
- **Year:** 2026

## What it is

A format sample: a short IMDb-style mini bio, a longer mini bio, and a 45-minute podcast interview run-of-show. The subject is Buster Keaton, a historical public figure, chosen so the format can be shown without using a living client's private details. Facts are drawn from public sources and labeled where a story might be legend.

## Who it's for

IMDb or press-bio writing, podcast booking or episode-prep, and "write my bio and a show outline" asks. For a real client, the same two pieces would be built only from their credits and notes.

## Key features

- ~100-word short bio written to be read aloud
- ~330-word full mini bio in a factual, third-person style
- A timed podcast structure with a goal, three talking points and segment prompts
- Legends flagged as possibly apocryphal

## Honesty

Illustrative sample about a public historical figure, not a client biography. Do not imply Alex worked with the Keaton estate, a studio, or a podcast that interviewed him. Do not reuse this structure with a living private person unless that person is the buyer and has supplied the facts.
