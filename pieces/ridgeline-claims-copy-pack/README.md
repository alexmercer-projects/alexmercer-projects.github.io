- **Category:** Writing / B2B copy

## What it is

A short-form copy pack for a fictional claims-workflow SaaS, Ridgeline Claims. It shows range across a cold-to-warm email, a landing-page hero, social posts and a sales-sheet style block, written in AP style for a VP of Claims or COO at a regional property insurer.

## Who it's for

B2B SaaS copy, insurance or claims-tech marketing, and "send a few examples of emails and web copy" asks.

## Key features

- Outbound marketing email (~140 words) with subject and preheader
- Landing-page hero and proof strip
- Additional short-form pieces in the same voice (see the PDF)
- Clearly labeled as a spec sample, not client work
