# Thistledown Coffee Roasters wholesale prospect list

- **Category:** Lead research
- **Label:** Concept demo
- **Year:** 2026

## What it is

A researched B2B lead list for a made-up coffee roaster looking for wholesale accounts inside its delivery area: 40 independent cafes, bakeries, restaurants, offices, gyms and grocers, each with a contact, a fit score, the reason it fits, a first line for outreach, and the page the details came from.

## Who it's for

Lead list and prospect research asks, web research into a spreadsheet, "find me businesses that could buy from us", and anyone who wants to see how a list is checked before they pay for one.

## Key features

- Six written rules a place has to pass before it goes on the list
- 40 rows you can search, filter by type, tier or town, and sort
- A fit score out of 10 from five visible parts, with a worked example
- Every row linked to its source with a checked date; no guessed emails
- 7 duplicates merged and 9 places left off, each with the rule it failed
- An Excel workbook with formulas, a Status dropdown and a summary tab, plus a CSV

## Honesty

Concept demo. Thistledown Coffee Roasters, its towns, and every business, person, address, phone number and email are made up. Phones are 555-01xx, emails and source links use example.com. Not client work. Do not claim meetings, sales or any results.
