# Pellory Ceramics: a 30-day Instagram storytelling plan

- **Category:** Social media
- **Label:** Concept demo
- **Year:** 2026

## What it is

The Instagram plan I'd hand the owner of a made-up one-person pottery studio for her next 30 days. It's built around her own story (a land surveyor who started throwing pots in a rented garage) and points at three goals: filling a Thursday wheel class, a December kiln-opening drop, and newsletter sign-ups. Everything is shown the way it would look on a phone: Reels as timed storyboards, carousels as real designed slides, captions with their length checked, and a Story sequence with its stickers.

## Who it's for

Small brands and personal brands looking for a social media manager, Instagram storytelling and Reels planning, caption writing, and content calendars.

## Key features

- A 30-day calendar (Monday start) with daily Stories, 12 Reels and 6 carousels across four content pillars, filterable by pillar and format
- A voice guide: four traits with "sounds like" and "not like" lines, words to use and avoid, an emoji rule and a caption formula
- Six Reel storyboards, shot by shot: timing, framing, on-screen text inside the safe zone, voiceover, edit notes, the first-second hook and the cover text
- Three carousels designed as HTML and CSS slides at 4:5, with swipe and arrow controls
- Nine captions with the line before "more" kept under 125 characters, the full caption under 2,200, 3 to 5 hashtags and alt text
- A seven-frame Story countdown to the kiln opening with poll, quiz, question, countdown, slider and link stickers
- A measurement plan with a weekly review checklist and three tests with decision rules; no results are claimed
- All content lives in one JSON file, checked by a Python script with 28 tests, and printed to a 24-page PDF
