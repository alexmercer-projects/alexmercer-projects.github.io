# Roofing claim and job tracker

- **Category:** Spreadsheet
- **Label:** Concept demo / template
- **Year:** 2026

## What it is

An Excel job tracker for a roofing contractor who works insurance claims, plus a short homeowner leave-behind that explains the hail-claim process in plain language. The workbook has a dashboard, a jobs tab and a how-to-use sheet. Sample rows are labeled SAMPLE, with names like "SAMPLE - Smith" at "123 Example St."

## Who it's for

Roofing and insurance-restoration contractors who need one place to follow jobs from inspection to close, and who want a clear handout for homeowners after a hail storm. The leave-behind also notes a few Colorado consumer rules at a high level.

## Key features

- Dashboard counts (jobs, inspections with damage, approvals, denials, contracts)
- Jobs sheet meant to be filled left to right
- Formula-driven flags described in the how-to sheet (follow-up, rescission window as a date-level reminder)
- Leave-behind: the claim path, a 5-step "what happens next," homeowner rights notes, and an FAQ
- Sample rows only; no real homeowner data

## Honesty

Sample and template. Rows marked SAMPLE are fictional placeholders. The leave-behind is general information, not legal or insurance advice, and it is not written for a named contractor. Do not claim this tracker is in use at a real roofing company or that it improved close rates.
