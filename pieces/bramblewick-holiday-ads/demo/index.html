How I’d set it up and judge it

A plan, not a report. Nothing here was run, so there are no numbers to show.

01

Two ad sets, not ten

New customers: broad targeting inside the shop’s shipping area, ages 25 and up, ads 01–03. Small budgets need fewer, bigger groups so Meta can learn.

Warm: site visitors from the last 30 days, page and Instagram engagers from the last 60 days, and the customer list. Ads 04–06.

02

Tracking before spend

Pixel and Conversions API sending purchase events with order value, checked with a test order. Every ad gets its own UTM tags, so the shop’s own sales report agrees with Ads Manager, or shows where it doesn’t.

03

One change at a time

Image fixed, primary text A against B against C, for the first week. Then the winning text with headline variants. Judged on cost per purchase once each variant has had a fair share of spend, not on clicks or likes.

04

Twice-weekly check

Monday and Thursday: pause what’s clearly behind, move budget to what’s ahead, and watch frequency in the warm set. When the same people keep seeing an ad, swap in a fresh variant from the bank rather than raise the budget.

05

Date-driven switches

Ad 05 goes live five days before the ship-by date and stops the night it passes. Ad 06 replaces it the next morning. Each change goes on a shared calendar so nobody is editing ads on Christmas Eve.

06

What gets handed over

Six 1440 × 1800 images, the copy bank in a spreadsheet, the UTM list, this calendar, and a one-page weekly note: what ran, what it cost, what changes next and why.
