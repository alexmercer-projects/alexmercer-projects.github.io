# Bramblewick Gift Co.: holiday Facebook ads

- **Category:** Ads
- **Label:** Concept demo
- **Year:** 2026

## What it is

A holiday Facebook ad set for a made-up gift shop: six 4:5 feed ads with images, three copy options for each line checked against Meta's recommended lengths, a run calendar through Christmas, and a test plan. The ads never ran.

## Who it's for

Facebook and Instagram ad posting or management gigs, holiday and small-shop e-commerce promos, and "show me ads you've made" asks.

## Key features

- Six finished ad images in `demo/creatives/` (1440x1800 JPEGs)
- Copy bank: 18 primary-text and 18 headline variants
- Flight calendar from late November to December 24, with Black Friday, Cyber Monday and a ship-by date marked
- A setup and testing plan (two ad sets, one change at a time, twice-weekly checks)
- One-page PDF of the set

## Honesty

Concept demo. The shop is not real and the ads never ran, so there is no spend, ROAS, sales or engagement to show. Products, prices and dates are placeholders. Do not invent results.
