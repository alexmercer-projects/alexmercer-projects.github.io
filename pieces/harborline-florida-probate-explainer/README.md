# Harborline Estate & Probate: Florida probate explainer

- **Category:** Legal writing
- **Label:** Concept demo
- **Year:** 2026

## What it is

A plain-English practice-area article for a made-up Florida estate and probate firm: "Do I have to go through probate in Florida? Summary vs. formal administration, explained." It covers what probate reaches and what skips it, the three paths (no administration, summary, formal), how the $150,000 summary limit is counted, homestead and exempt property, and creditor deadlines. Every rule is linked to the 2026 Florida Statutes or the probate rule it comes from, and the July 1, 2026 change from $75,000 to $150,000 is flagged.

## Who it's for

Law-firm content and blog writing asks, estate planning and probate practice-area pages, and legal explainers that need to be accurate, sourced and readable by non-lawyers.

## Key features

- About 1,870 words of body copy at a 7.4 Flesch-Kincaid grade
- SEO title, meta description and a keyword plan
- A short-answer box and an at-a-glance panel
- A five-step "which path fits" decision flow
- A comparison table that stacks into cards on a phone
- Five FAQs with FAQPage structured data
- A Florida Bar-aware call to action and a "not legal advice" disclaimer
- Writer's notes on reading level, keywords, compliance and sources
- A 9-page PDF of the article

## Honesty

Concept demo. The firm, its phone number and its email are made up, and this is not client work. It has not been reviewed by a Florida-licensed attorney and is not legal advice. Do not claim it was published, ranked or brought in clients.
