# The Pope Who Was Put on Trial Nine Months After He Died

- **Category:** History writing
- **Label:** Spec sample
- **Year:** 2026

## What it is

An unpublished ~600-word history short about the Cadaver Synod of 897, when Pope Stephen VI put the exhumed body of Pope Formosus on trial. It is a narrative anecdote written from public historical sources, meant to show how Alex writes history for a general reader.

## Who it's for

History, anecdote and narrative nonfiction asks, short articles for a general audience, and "write me a sample on a historical episode" requests.

## Key features

- A cold open that puts the reader in the Lateran Palace
- The political reason under the legal charges
- A complete short arc (trial, verdict, aftermath) in about 600 words

## Honesty

Unpublished spec sample, not a commissioned article and not a published clip. Do not list a magazine or site as the publisher. The events are historical; the piece is Alex's writing, not a client's manuscript.
