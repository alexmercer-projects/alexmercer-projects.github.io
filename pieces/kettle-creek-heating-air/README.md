# Kettle Creek Heating & Air: short-form ad scripts

- **Category:** Writing / ads
- **Label:** Concept demo
- **Year:** 2026

## What it is

A hook bank and three timed 9:16 short-form video ad scripts for a made-up heating and air company. Twelve hooks across four angles, plus the plan for how the ads would be tested. The ads were never shot or run.

## Who it's for

Direct-response ad script asks, UGC or short-form video hooks, Meta and TikTok creative, and home-services marketing copy. Useful when a buyer says "show me ads you've written" or wants hooks before a shoot.

## Key features

- Four angles: the 2 a.m. click; price, out loud; show the dirt; the tech, not the van
- Twelve opening lines, each with a first shot
- Three full timed scripts, including "58 degrees," "What we won't sell you," and "Working fine"
- A test plan
- One-page PDF of the pack

## Honesty

Concept demo. The company is made up and the ads never ran, so there are no results, spend, or ROAS figures. Names, prices and the phone number are placeholders. Do not invent performance claims.
