# Linden Field Psychology

- **Category:** Website
- **Label:** Concept demo
- **Year:** 2026

## What it is

A four-page site for a made-up psychology practice in a fictional town called Merrinford. It covers home, services, about and getting started, with intake through an outside portal placeholder (nothing health-related is collected on the page), telehealth-only sessions, office hours and a 988 crisis line in the footer.

## Who it's for

Small professional-service websites, especially therapy or wellness practices that should not take health information through the site itself.

## Key features

- Four linked pages with a sticky header, a phone menu and a booking bar
- Home page with a cloud film, service rows, hours and a short introduction
- Services page with individual, couples and group offerings plus example specialties
- About page with a placeholder portrait, demo credentials and a short made-up bio
- Getting started steps that point at a placeholder patient portal, plus common questions
- A 988 crisis line in the footer, and a note asking people not to send health information by email
- Works on a phone, with self-hosted fonts

## Honesty

Concept demo. Linden Field Psychology and Dr. Rowan Linden are not real. Names, credentials, the town, the phone number, the email and the portal link are placeholders. The site does not collect health information, and the appointment buttons are for show.
