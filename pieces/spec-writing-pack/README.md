# Spec writing pack (blog, email and SEO page)

- **Category:** Writing
- **Label:** Spec samples
- **Year:** 2026

## What it is

Three unpublished writing samples, written to show style and range. None are client work, and none are written for or endorsed by a named company.

1. **Blog post:** "How to Write an Artist Statement Collectors Actually Read" (~600 words), for visual artists selling their own work, with a before-and-after example.
2. **Email and social:** a ~200-word newsletter email plus three social posts, repurposed from that blog post.
3. **SEO category page:** "Impact-Resistant Shingles" (~350 words) for homeowners, aimed at the keyword "impact resistant shingles," with a clear next step.

## Who it's for

Blog, newsletter, social and SEO-page writing asks, content packs that need one idea told three ways, and contractor or e-commerce category copy.

## Key features

- Plain, specific writing with a before-and-after in the artist-statement piece
- An email that restates the same idea in a shorter form
- A category page that explains a product, sets honest expectations on cost and insurance, and does not invent discounts

## Honesty

Spec samples. Not published and not client work. The "Request a free roof inspection" close on the shingles page is example CTA copy, not a real contractor offer. Do not attribute these to a magazine, shop or agency.
