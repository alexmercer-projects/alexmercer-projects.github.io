# Noa Brightwater resume and LinkedIn makeover

- **Category:** Writing
- **Label:** Concept demo
- **Year:** 2026

## What it is

A before-and-after resume makeover for a made-up operations manager. The old resume is two columns with a photo, skill bars, an objective and duty bullets. The new one is a single-column, ATS-friendly page with standard headings, one date format and results in every bullet, plus a rewritten LinkedIn headline and About section.

## Who it's for

Resume formatting and rewriting asks, "make my resume ATS-friendly", LinkedIn profile rewrites, and anyone who wants to see a sample before handing over their own resume.

## Key features

- Before and after at letter-page proportions, with eight numbered fixes explained
- Five bullet rewrites, from duties to results
- Keyword check against a sample posting, counted from the real text of each version
- LinkedIn headline and About section, before and after, with character counts
- Plain text pulled from the finished PDF to show it reads in order
- The new resume as a PDF and an editable Word file

## Honesty

Concept demo. Noa Brightwater, the employers, the school and the job posting are made up, and every number is example data. Not client work. Do not claim hires, interviews or any results.
