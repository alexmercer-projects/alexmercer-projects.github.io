# Thirty Seconds of Quiet: a feature film pitch deck

- **Category:** Pitch deck
- **Label:** Concept demo
- **Year:** 2026

## What it is

A 16-slide pitch deck I wrote and designed for a made-up romantic comedy feature. A sound editor has twelve days to record thirty seconds of silence in every room of her late mentor's Los Angeles house, and the estate-sale man emptying it will not stop talking. It reads as a web page, runs full screen in present mode, and prints to a 16:9 PDF.

## Who it's for

Writers, producers and small production companies who need a pitch deck or lookbook for a feature, a series or a short, and anyone who needs a story turned into a clear, good-looking presentation.

## Key features

- 16 slides: title, logline, premise, tone, a three-act synopsis, the two leads, the supporting cast, the world, look and feel, the sound approach, five trailer scenes, why now, audience, format and scale, a writer's note and a closing slide
- Tone shown with dials and plain-language comparisons instead of real film titles or names, so nothing implies talent is attached
- Every image is hand-built SVG and CSS: the title scene, character cards, a floor plan of the house, a mood board and a waveform
- Format and budget tier clearly marked as illustrative, with no budget figures, forecasts or box-office numbers
- Present mode with arrow keys, and a phone layout where each slide reflows into a readable card
- The same source prints to a 16-page 16:9 PDF with headless Chrome
