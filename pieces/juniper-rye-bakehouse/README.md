# Juniper & Rye Bakehouse

- **Category:** Website
- **Label:** Concept demo
- **Year:** 2026

## What it is

A one-page site for a made-up neighborhood bakery. It shows today's bake, a three-column menu (bread, pastry, coffee), preorders for pickup, hours and a find-us block. The headline is "Slow bread for fast mornings."

## Who it's for

Small-business website and landing-page asks, including bakery, cafe and other local retailers who want a simple, phone-friendly site. Also useful when a buyer wants to see "the kind of site you'd build" before hiring.

## Key features

- Today's bake board with items and prices
- Full menu
- Preorder-for-pickup section
- Hours and location block
- Self-hosted fonts (Fraunces, DM Sans)
- Concept-demo labeling in the page title and description

## Honesty

This is a concept demo for a bakery that does not exist. Names, prices and contact details are placeholders. Do not claim it is a live business, a client project, or that it produced sales, traffic or reviews.
